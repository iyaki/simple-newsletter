# Privacy Policy — Simple Newsletter

Last updated: 2026-09-08

## 1. Who we are

Simple Newsletter is a free RSS/Atom-to-email service operated by iyaki. For any privacy question, contact <simple-newsletter@iyaki.ar>.

## 2. What data we collect

- **Subscription data:** your email address, the feed URI you subscribe to, and the timestamps of your confirmation and subscription.
- **Security logs:** our web server may briefly process your IP address to serve requests and prevent abuse.
- **Analytics:** we use [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a cookieless, privacy-friendly analytics service that collects aggregated, non-identifying page-view statistics.

We do not collect names, passwords, payment data, or any browsing profile. The Service sets no cookies.

## 3. What we use it for

- To deliver the newsletters you requested: confirming your subscription (double opt-in) and emailing you new posts from the feeds you subscribed to.
- To let you manage your subscription through the links included in every email.
- To operate, secure and improve the Service.

## 4. Legal basis (GDPR)

Processing of your email address is based on your **consent**, given explicitly through the double opt-in confirmation, and on the **performance of the requested service**. Security logging relies on our **legitimate interest** in keeping the Service safe and abuse-free.

## 5. Who we share data with

We never sell your data. We share the minimum necessary with the providers that operate the Service:

- **Hosting / email delivery:** the infrastructure provider and SMTP relay that send the newsletters on our behalf.
- **Analytics:** GoatCounter (aggregated statistics only).

Your email address is used solely to deliver your subscriptions and is not shared with feed publishers or any third party beyond the providers listed above.

## 6. Data retention

We keep your subscription data while the subscription is active. When you cancel via the link in any email, your subscription — including your email address — is removed immediately. Aggregated analytics contain no personal data.

## 7. Your rights

You can unsubscribe from any newsletter at any time using the cancellation link included in every email, which deletes your data immediately. You may also request access, correction, portability or erasure of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to <simple-newsletter@iyaki.ar>.

## 8. Security

The Service is served over HTTPS, uses hashed confirmation tokens, collects the minimum data needed to function, and applies rate limiting to prevent abuse. Source code is publicly available at [GitHub](https://github.com/iyaki/simple-newsletter) for anyone to audit.

## 9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always published on this page with its last-updated date.
